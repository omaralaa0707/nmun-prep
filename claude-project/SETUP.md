# Setting up the mock-interview Project on claude.ai (about 5 minutes)

## 1. Create the Project (on your Mac, at claude.ai)
1. Go to **claude.ai → Projects → Create project**.
2. Name: **NMUN Interview Coach**. Description (optional): "Mock selection panel and speaking coach for NMUN 2027."
3. Open **Instructions** (sometimes labelled "Set project instructions") and paste everything from `INSTRUCTIONS.md`.
4. In **Project knowledge**, click **+** / **Add content → Upload files** and add these four files from this folder:
   - `01_un_core.md`
   - `02_question_bank.md`
   - `03_nmun_2027_committees.md`
   - `04_my_profile.md`

## 2. Practise by voice (iPhone)
1. Open the **Claude app**, go to **Projects → NMUN Interview Coach**, and start a new chat **inside the project**.
2. Tap the **voice** button (the sound-wave icon next to the message box) to talk hands-free.
3. Say one of these to start:
   - "Full mock, 30 minutes."
   - "Full mock, 60 minutes. Be tough."
   - "Rapid-fire on the Security Council."
   - "Deep dive on the ICJ."
   - "Follow-up ladder: why did the League of Nations fail?"
   - "Explain it back: I'll teach you R2P."
   - "Answer polish: tell us about yourself."
4. Say **"pause"** to step out and ask a question, **"resume"** to continue, and **"end"** for your scorecard.
5. After a full mock, say **"yes"** when asked for the written scorecard, so you have it on screen to save.

You can do the same on your Mac by typing or dictating, but voice on the phone is closest to the real interview.

## 3. Keep the Project up to date
- **After each session:** add the suggested line to the *Session log* in `04_my_profile.md`, then replace the file in Project knowledge. That way the next session targets your weak spots.
- **When delegations are announced:** fill in your delegation, committees and topics in `04_my_profile.md`. Then ask Claude Code (this chat) to build the country and topic files; it will add `05_country.md` and `06_topics.md` here for you to upload.

## Suggested rhythm until 14 November
- **Every day:** one 10–15 minute voice session (rapid-fire or deep dive on what you read that day).
- **Every 2–3 days:** a full mock. 30 minutes until about 5 November, then 45–60 minutes.
- **12–13 November:** one full 60-minute mock each day, in "be tough" mode.
